# Hord

Hord is a personal library for the movies, series, books and artworks you love — on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy)
- Support and questions: **hord.app@outlook.com**

Movie and series data and images are provided by [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Book data comes from Google Books and Open Library.
